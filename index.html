About WasteXchange

WasteXchange is a simple platform to connect people and businesses who want to buy, sell, or exchange recyclable waste materials. It helps reduce landfill waste and promotes a circular economy.

  • Post your recyclable waste for sale or exchange.
  • Browse listings by category, location, and type.
  • Contact sellers directly via phone, WhatsApp, or email.

Note: This demo version stores data only in your browser (local storage). For a real deployment, connect this frontend to a backend and database.
